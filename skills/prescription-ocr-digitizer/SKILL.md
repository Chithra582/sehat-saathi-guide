# Prescription OCR Digitizer Skill

## Overview
Processes digital photos of medical prescriptions to extract structured medication lists, dosage instructions, and schedules while safeguarding patient privacy.

## Operations
1. Preprocesses prescription images using contrast enhancement and skew correction algorithms.
2. Extracts text tokens and validates medicine names against verified pharmaceutical catalogs.
3. Converts dosage frequencies into automated daily reminder schedules.
4. Redacts patient names, doctor registration numbers, and hospital identifiers for data privacy.
