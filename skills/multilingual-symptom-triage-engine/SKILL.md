# Multilingual Symptom Triage Engine Skill

## Overview
Analyzes user-reported health symptoms across Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Bhojpuri, Maithili, and English, determining clinical urgency and providing protocol-driven triage advice.

## Operations
1. Evaluates reported symptoms against high-acuity emergency red flags (stroke, cardiac arrest, respiratory failure).
2. Categorizes urgency into Emergency, Urgent PHC Visit, Routine Consultation, or Home Self-Care.
3. Translates medical concepts into culturally appropriate, empathetic vernacular phrasing.
4. Generates triage summary reports with explicit non-diagnostic disclaimers and emergency hotline numbers.
